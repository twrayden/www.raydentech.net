<section class="page-card">
	<h2>AI Features Built Into Real Software</h2>
	<p>We help teams add AI capabilities to real applications and internal tools without turning the work into a research project. Our focus is on the application layer: integrating managed AI services, wiring them into business workflows, and building the surrounding services needed to make them reliable in production.</p>
	<ul>
		<li><strong>Managed AI Service Integrations:</strong> Build against platforms such as AWS Bedrock and similar hosted AI services.</li>
		<li><strong>Application Layer Development:</strong> Create the APIs, application services, queues, jobs, and orchestration needed to support AI-powered features.</li>
		<li><strong>Workflow Automation:</strong> Connect AI capabilities into existing systems, approvals, notifications, and business processes.</li>
		<li><strong>Data &amp; Context Integration:</strong> Feed the right internal data, documents, or system context into AI-enabled workflows.</li>
		<li><strong>Operational Reliability:</strong> Handle monitoring, error recovery, usage controls, and cost-aware implementation choices.</li>
	</ul>
	<p>We focus on practical implementation: integrating AI services into products, internal tools, and automation pipelines. We are not positioning this service around model training or design-led AI demos.</p>
</section>
