<section class="page-card">
	<h2>Financial Software Built for Technical Reliability</h2>
	<p>We build software for quantitative finance, trading workflows, finance-related automation, and selected crypto or blockchain-related systems where the main challenge is technical execution. That can include data ingestion, strategy support tooling, trade execution pipelines, monitoring, wallet or exchange integrations, and connections to broker or market-data APIs. Depending on the use case, that may mean streaming event-driven architecture for higher-frequency trading workflows or scheduled job architecture for swing-trading and other time-based processes.</p>
	<ul>
		<li><strong>IBKR API Integrations:</strong> Connect with Interactive Brokers to retrieve market data, place trades, and manage positions programmatically.</li>
		<li><strong>Exchange Integrations:</strong> Integrate with platforms such as BTC Markets for market data, order handling, and account or position workflows.</li>
		<li><strong>Trading Automation:</strong> Build services and workflows that support signal processing, order routing, execution logic, and the right runtime model for either real-time or scheduled strategies.</li>
		<li><strong>Portfolio &amp; Position Management:</strong> Track positions, balances, fills, and portfolio state across internal systems.</li>
		<li><strong>Market Data Pipelines:</strong> Collect, normalise, store, and serve pricing or reference data for downstream systems.</li>
		<li><strong>Crypto &amp; Blockchain Development:</strong> Build supporting software, integrations, and operational tooling around crypto platforms or blockchain-related workflows.</li>
		<li><strong>Operational Tooling:</strong> Create dashboards, alerts, reconciliation tools, and internal utilities for trading or finance teams.</li>
	</ul>
	<p>We focus on building the software and integration layer. We do not provide financial advice, strategy advice, or regulated investment services.</p>
</section>
